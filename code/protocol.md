# Neural operators for smoke in computer graphics
Synthetic 2D smoke, incompressible dynamics with buoyancy, periodic domain without obstacles. An FNO correction on a 48x48 grid directly modifies density and vorticity after every limited MacCormack step. The corrected states feed all following frames. A classical 144x144 simulation with four substeps produces the numerical reference; it is not an experimental measurement.
Independent scenes per seed: 88 for training, 12 to validate the checkpoint with a rollout, 24 for testing (12 with wind in the training range, 12 with stronger wind). 40 frames, interval 0.045. The model is trained on correction steps starting from reference states reduced to 48x48. Epochs and checkpoint are defined in the code. The test scenes are never used to select the weights.
Classical MacCormack references with 48, 64, 96 and 144 cells per side and one substep per frame. An additional comparison at 48 cells with four substeps. All images are brought to 144x144. Warm CPU measurements, order permuted per scene, median/p95 of the times per frame, including the output interpolation and excluding video writing and rendering.
Operational progress criterion: mean density RMSE reduced by at least 5% relative to the classical reference with the lowest error among those with a median latency of at most 115% of the hybrid method, a paired per-scene bootstrap interval entirely above zero, and at least 2 times faster than the 144x144 reference with four substeps. Also report the 48x48 results, the gradient errors and the outcome with stronger wind. The videos are illustrative.
