# Check against a finer reference
A new check motivated by the 192x192 sensitivity comparison on eight scenes of the previous phase. The linear temporal 96x96 solver with midpoint and the already selected eight-coefficient filter (strength 0.5) is frozen. The same spectral response is factorised into two tables, one per axis. No retraining. The numerical equivalence of the step is checked on the development scenes.
64 independent scenes are tested, seeds 7000–7063, 32 with normal wind and 32 with wind beyond the training range. All densities are compared at 144x144, with area averaging for the 192x192 reference with eight substeps. The previous 144x144 reference with four substeps remains as a parallel analysis on the same sample. A finer diagnostic, defined before the test: 256x256 with 16 substeps on seeds 7000–7003 and 7032–7035.
The classical numerical comparisons of the previous phase are used, with additional linear temporal grids 88, 100, 108, 116 and 120. Order permuted per scene, warm CPU, latency of the step and of the output interpolation. Primary: at least 5% lower mean RMSE and a positive stratified paired interval against the best classical method within 115% of the median latency of the variant, and at least 2x relative to the fine reference. Secondary, stated separately: relative to the 112x112 temporal classical method, at least 10% saving in median latency with a paired interval on the relative error increase entirely within +5%. This last comparison represents a specific point on the quality–latency curve, with both wind regimes. No criterion assesses GPU rendering or 3D scenes.
